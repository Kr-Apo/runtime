a claim that the whole application is GPL. Embedded fonts retain their MIT,

Browser dependencies retain their MIT, ISC or BSD-3-Clause notices.

Keep this source-availability notice alongside the release notes when sharing
the ZIP. It supplements publication information; it does not change any tested
archive bytes or substitute licenses. No FFmpeg executable is distributed.
